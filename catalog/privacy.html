UnrealDB: privacy and cookies

UnrealDB is a public, view-only catalogue. Visitors do not need accounts or logins; only administrators sign in. We process technical access information to operate the site, investigate errors and protect it against automated scraping. We do not operate an optional visitor-analytics service or use optional visitor-tracking cookies.

Essential security processing

The web server and security tools process IP addresses, requested URLs, request times, user-agent strings, response codes, and attack indicators. These are needed for service reliability, abuse detection and incident response; they are not optional analytics cookies.

The three-second direct-entry verification uses the HttpOnly cookies UEDB_GATE_ISSUE (up to five minutes) and UEDB_GATE_PASS (up to thirty minutes). These are strictly necessary security cookies; they identify a completed check, not a personal profile. UnrealDB is publicly readable without accounts or visitor logins. Technical session cookies may support normal browsing; only site administrators use authentication cookies. Completing the bot check does not create an account, sign you in, or identify you as a registered user.

No privacy popups or tracking choice cookies

UnrealDB does not ask visitors to consent to optional analytics because optional visitor analytics are not being collected. There is no compulsory privacy acknowledgement. Previously used UEDB_PRIVACY_NOTICE and UEDB_PRIVACY_CHOICE cookies are retired and no longer set.

Security information shared with CrowdSec

UnrealDB uses CrowdSec for security monitoring. Where CrowdSec community threat-intelligence sharing is active, CrowdSec's Central API can receive information about detected attack sources, including the offending IP address, alert scenario, time and security engine identifier. These are threat signals, not complete website browsing histories. An IP address may be personal data under GDPR. We do not sell visitor data or share it for targeted advertising. A statement that no data is shared with any third party would be inaccurate.

Privacy rights and contact

Security records are processed separately from any consent choice, for service protection and operational diagnostics. GDPR rights, including access or erasure where applicable, can be exercised by contacting the website administrator. Please do not include passwords in requests.
