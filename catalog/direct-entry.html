Manual access verification

UnrealDB is experiencing continuous automated scraping and bot traffic. To keep the site available for visitors, direct links to resource-intensive catalogue pages require a short manual verification.

No account or login is needed. This check may also appear when opening a bookmark or when your browser omits referral information. File Examine links require verification once per browser, even when opened from search results, because referral information can be forged. Other catalogue pages may recognise trusted referrals.
